How Road Vikas Works

Three simple steps to make your voice heard.

1. Spot an Issue

Notice a pothole, damaged road, or another problem affecting your locality.

2. Submit a Report

Tell us the state, city, locality, road name, address, and details of the issue.

3. Help Drive Change

Your report creates a clear record of the problem and helps highlight areas that need attention.
